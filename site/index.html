About the data & sources
    The site rebuilds every week from these sources; the 1968–2004 history is rebuilt only when NCHS or NBER revise their files. Every figure comes from a source table; MortalGIS adds no estimates. Counts of 1–9 deaths are hidden, as CDC does. Medical examiner cases are grouped into ~1 km squares and ZIP Codes so no single home can be picked out.
