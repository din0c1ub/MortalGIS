About the data & sources
    The site rebuilds every week from these sources; the 1959–2004 history is rebuilt only when NCHS, NBER or ICPSR revise their files. Every figure comes from a source table; MortalGIS adds no estimates. Counts of 1–9 deaths are hidden, as CDC does, except where a state health department publishes them (outlined with dashes; the tooltip names the state source). A few states publish only that a county had 1–4 deaths; the tooltip of a hidden county then says so, and the count and rate stay hidden. The 5-year totals switch shows most small counties. Medical examiner cases are grouped into ~1 km squares and ZIP Codes so no single home can be picked out.
    Alabama counts: These data were provided by the Alabama Department of Public Health. The Department specifically disclaims responsibility for any analyses, interpretations, or conclusions.
    North Carolina counts: the original source is the N.C. State Center for Health Statistics, shown unaltered.
